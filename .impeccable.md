## Design Context

### Users
Mobile listeners who stream free music from Jamendo and Audius, play audio stored on the device, and keep favorites and playlists locally.

### Brand Personality
Calm, nocturnal and polished — a violet "after hours" music room rather than a utility screen.

### Aesthetic Direction
Source of truth: `Music Stream Play Launcher.pen` (pen.dev). One dark canvas (#101014) with a continuous lilac/violet atmosphere at the top, rounded grouped cards with hairline borders, lilac (#C9BDE8) as the single accent, Inter throughout, Material Symbols (rounded) for every glyph. The UI is built with Jetpack Compose and Material 3 components themed to these tokens (`ui/theme`).

### Design Principles
1. Playback state is always visible: the active song is lilac with an equalizer, the mini player floats above content.
2. Group related rows in one card; dividers start under the text column, never under artwork.
3. Every state has a designed surface — loading, empty, permission and error panels with one clear action.
4. Animate transform/opacity only (player flip, sheet and page transitions) and honour "Remove animations".
5. Touch targets are at least 48dp and every icon-only control has a content description.
